## 3. Gates and interpretation

Stable LaTeX insertion anchors: H01 `sec:measurementmethods` / `sec:measurement`;
H02 `sec:action` / `sec:replication`; H03 `sec:ranking` / `sec:decisionresults`;
H04 `sec:numerics` / `app:numerics`. Prefer these labels over mutable page/section numbers.

**H01.** Use only histories available at prediction time; future/oracle observations remain separately labeled. Lock representation surfaces, capacity comparisons, and readout selection on development/validation roots, then evaluate untouched fresh roots. Support requires both absolute-state and paired-effect resolution adequate for the proposed edit scale, not merely superiority to a mean baseline. Qualify actual-history, predicted, and edited surfaces separately. Failure narrows the tested readout route; inconclusiveness leaves temporal rescue unresolved, not information absent. Passing does not retroactively qualify v0 editors.

**H02.** Require H01-qualified surface-specific measurement or an independently qualified alternative. Predefine simulator-state/contact contrasts and pusher/block controls on development roots; test fresh roots without outcome-based exclusions. Compare matched action effects with zero-change and wrong assignments while separating pusher and block contributions. Support requires direction-sensitive, practically resolved block effects across the declared state contrasts, not global latent sensitivity. Pusher-dominated outcomes restrict the physical interpretation of F3 without erasing replicated latent fidelity. Unresolved measurement makes H02 inconclusive, not false.

**H03.** Design contact/reachable candidate banks using development-only choices and deployable information; freeze them before fresh-root evaluation. Treat simulator support labels and best-candidate outcomes as privileged diagnostics, never outcome-based retention rules. Report all banks and predeclared support strata, including zero-coverage banks. Support for useful decisions requires meaningful reachable outcomes and improvement over hold with uncertainty; separately compare encoded-endpoint and forecast selection to distinguish metric limitations from forecasting. No coverage means inadequate decision support; adequate coverage with poor endpoint ranking implicates the tested metric. Inconclusive contrasts identify neither mechanism nor control success. A redesigned bank is a new support, not retroactive confirmation.

**H04.** Proceed only if a proposed claim or deployment depends on unresolved cross-process/device/layout portability. Select workloads/backends separately from a fresh locked audit workload. Predeclare claim-relevant tolerances and test paired contrasts, not just aggregate MSE. Passing bounds tested portability only; failure restricts the claim; inconclusiveness retains uncertainty. Completed N01–N02 are not automatically repeated, and reduced drift is not improved physical accuracy.

## 4. Evidence governance

Any separately authorized study needs a prospective frozen protocol specifying estimands, controls, roster separation, budget/stopping, practical gates, and multiplicity/uncertainty before collection. No adaptive seed or sample extension to chase significance; failures and inconclusive outcomes remain reportable.

Archive v0 unchanged. Version v1+ by evidence checkpoint with provenance, added-result ledger, and explicit claim changes—not switches to favorable storytelling. Retain v0 failures in main-text synthesis and appendices, distinguish discovery from fresh evaluation, and never promote secondary findings into primary successes retrospectively.
